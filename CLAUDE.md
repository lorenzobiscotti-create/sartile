# Sartile — Project Context

> This file gives Claude full context on the Sartile project. Read this before writing any code, making any design decision, or suggesting anything.

---

## What Is Sartile

Sartile is an AI-powered clothing brand mentor and co-partner for teens and young adults (16–25) who want to start a clothing brand from zero. It is not a checklist tool, not a course, and not a one-time dashboard. It is an active business partner that lives inside the founder's growth — from the moment they have an idea, through their first sale, and deep into their scaling journey.

**Tagline:** Where brands are born.

**Domain:** sartile.com

**The name:** From *sartorial* — the art and craft of tailoring and clothing. Sartile is the platform built for the people who live and breathe what they wear.

---

## The Problem

Every day thousands of teens and young adults decide they want to start a clothing brand. They have the vision, the aesthetic, and the drive. What they don't have is a roadmap or anyone to walk it with them. They burn hours on YouTube tutorials that contradict each other, buy overpriced courses that give generic advice, set up stores wrong, target the wrong audience, and waste money on products that don't sell because nobody told them why.

---

## Target Customer

- Age: 16–25
- Mindset: wants to skip the 9-to-5, building something that reflects them
- Already spending money on tools and courses that don't work
- Lives on TikTok and Instagram

## The Four Acts

### Act 1 — The Mentor (Student tier)
From zero to first sale. Sartile meets the user where they are and walks them forward.

- Adaptive onboarding — chip-selection question flow (platform, production, audience, content, design stage)
- Personalized audience profile: age, platforms, content style, price sensitivity
- Step-by-step setup path through Printify, Shopify, Canva, Namelix, and more
- Marketing plan tailored to their specific brand and demographic
- UGC connector — links to Fiverr, Billo, JoinBrands to get first content made
- Tool recommendations at the right moment, not all at once
- Always-open mentor chat — ask anything, get answers in full context of their brand
- Multiple roadmaps supported — each roadmap is a separate brand/project with its own chat history

### Act 2 — The Partner (Founder + Partner tiers)
Inside the growth, not watching from outside.

- Live dashboard pulling sales, traffic, growth, and engagement from Shopify and social platforms
- AI reads the data and tells them what it means and exactly what to do about it
- Proactive check-ins — Sartile shows up first, user asks follow-up questions
- Exact posting windows based on when their specific audience is active
- Ad guidance — where to run ads, what format, what budget for their stage
- Arcad.ai integration for AI-powered ad creation at the right moment
- Milestone check-ins: you hit $500/month — here is what brands at $2k do differently
- Competitor analysis and trend alerts specific to their niche
- 30-day content calendar refreshed monthly

### Act 3 — The Label (Label tier)
Running a serious brand, not just starting one.

- 150 messages/day — near-unlimited for active founders
- 30 image uploads/day — send sarti anything: products, designs, competitor screenshots
- AI image generation — 5 generated images/day via DALL-E 3 (design concepts, mockup ideas, visual briefs)
- Weekly brand report — automated sarti summary of the week's data and priorities
- Influencer outreach scripts — sarti writes personalized DMs and emails for creator partnerships

### Act 4 — The Ecosystem (Phase 3)
A network that works for them.

- Phase 1: UGC connector links to Fiverr, Billo, JoinBrands — affiliate revenue on every referral
- Phase 3: In-app UGC creator marketplace — creators come to Sartile to find brand deals
- Brands post briefs, creators apply, content gets made inside the platform
- Creators pay to get featured to more brands, brands pay to access more creators
- Sartile takes a cut — third revenue stream

## The Four Entry Points

Users enter at one of four stages. Sartile detects where they are and skips everything they've already done.

1. **Just an Idea** — has a direction but no design yet. Sartile helps define the concept, recommends Namelix for brand naming, connects them to designers and AI design tools.
2. **Has a Design** — artwork ready. Skip Step 1. Move to mockup creation, product selection, audience profiling.
3. **Has a Mockup** — product visualized. Focus shifts to store setup, pricing, platform selection, marketing plan.
4. **Ready to Sell** — everything set. Sartile builds the launch strategy — where to post, what to say, when, which platforms matter most.

---

## The Mentor Chat

Sartile is always proactive — check-ins, alerts, milestones, recommendations. It shows up first. The chat is always open for the user to ask anything at any time. Every answer is generated with full context of their brand, their data, and their stage. Not a generic answer — their answer.

The AI never dumps all information at once. It introduces the right tool at the right moment in the journey.

**Sarti's voice:** Casual and direct. Like texting a friend who's built brands before. No "certainly", no "great question", no bullets. Short paragraphs. Reacts like a real person to progress updates. Every answer references their specific brand.

## Dashboard Panels (built, data connections pending Phase 2)

The app has four dashboard sections accessible from the sidebar:

- **Analytics** — connects to Shopify + Instagram + TikTok. Shows revenue, orders, visitors, conversion. sarti reads the data and tells you what to do. Currently shows placeholder state until store is connected.
- **Audience** — posting time heatmap (Mon–Sun × Morning/Afternoon/Evening/Night), age breakdown, competitor analysis (coming Phase 2). "Ask sarti" button on every section.
- **Designs** — tool directory: Canva, Adobe Express, Remove.bg, Printify mockups, Placeit, Smartmockups, Midjourney, DALL·E, Fiverr. Each card has an "Ask sarti" button that pre-fills the chat with a relevant prompt.
- **Marketing** — UGC tools (Fiverr, Billo, JoinBrands), Arcad.ai featured card, content tactics (TikTok organic, repurposing, founder-led content, packaging reveals). Each card has an "Ask sarti" button.

**"Ask sarti" button:** Every tool card and tactic card in all four panels has an "Ask sarti" chip. Clicking it switches to chat view and pre-fills the textarea with a specific, context-aware prompt for that tool.

## How Sartile Makes Money

### Subscriptions
Student (free) / Founder ($9/mo) / Partner ($19/mo) / Label ($39/mo)

### Affiliate Revenue
Every tool Sartile recommends has an affiliate program. Because Sartile is the first thing users interact with before setting anything up, every referral is a qualified high-intent lead.

- Printify — $25–40 per signup
- Printful — commission on first orders
- Shopify — up to $150 per merchant
- Canva Pro — recurring commission
- Fiverr — commission on designer hires
- Arcad.ai — affiliate on ad tool subscriptions
- Billo / JoinBrands — commission on UGC orders
- Namelix — check for affiliate program

### Marketplace (Phase 3)
UGC creator marketplace — Sartile takes a cut of transactions between brands and creators.

## AI Cost Structure

- **Chat model:** Claude Sonnet (not Opus — Sonnet is more than sufficient and 5x cheaper on output)
- Cost: ~$0.015 per full mentor session (onboarding)
- Cost: ~$0.001 per chat message
- 150 messages/day on Label tier = ~$0.15/day in API costs max
- **Image generation:** DALL-E 3 via OpenAI API — ~$0.04/image standard quality
- 5 generations/day on Label tier = $0.20/day max per user
- Shopify API: completely free
- Margins are healthy at all four tiers

## Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js 16 (App Router)
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS + CSS-in-JS (inline pageStyles string in components — do not switch this to Tailwind classes)
- **Database + Auth + Storage:** Supabase
- **Payments:** Stripe (Phase 2)
- **AI — Chat:** Anthropic API — Claude Sonnet (`claude-sonnet-4-6`)
- **AI — Image generation:** OpenAI API — DALL-E 3 (requires `OPENAI_API_KEY` in `.env.local`)
- **Hosting:** Vercel
- **Store data:** Shopify Partner API (free, Phase 2)
- **Social data:** Instagram Graph API, TikTok API (Phase 2)
- **Auth method:** Supabase Auth with Google OAuth

### Responsive priority
- Mobile first for landing page and onboarding — that's where the 16–25 audience lives
- Desktop first for dashboard and deeper product features

## Design Principles

- Not a checklist. Sartile goes on the journey with the user — always active, always proactive.
- The mentor drives the relationship. The chat is always open.
- Introduce tools at the right moment, not all at once.
- Every answer is personal — generated with full context of their brand.
- Mobile first for discovery and onboarding. Desktop first for the dashboard.
- The product should feel like a brand, not a SaaS tool.
- Target audience is 16–25 — design should feel premium and modern, not corporate.
- No generic startup UI. No hero text with colored accent words. No three-card feature section as the only content. Be different.
- Sarti talks like a real person — casual, direct, no corporate speak.
